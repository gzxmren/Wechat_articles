我用Muse做了20多件事，省下的时间够活十年

导读：Muse上线就爆了，下载量是ChatGPT同期的近三倍。一位科技爱好者分享了他使用Muse的技巧，Muse可盯他的屏幕、读他的邮件、查他的加密钱包。他还说：如果足够信任，会给它完全自主权。  

阅读难度：★★☆☆☆

另外，我们的打卡活动，如果打卡满了天数可以后台私信一下我们领取奖励。具体活动如下：阅读打卡挑战｜每天积累一点点，收获专属奖励

Since I started using Muse a couple weeks ago, I've probably spent 60+ hours with it. I see a lot of people wondering how to use Muse in productive ways.

自从几周前开始用Muse，我大概已经花了60多个小时在上面。我看到很多人想知道怎么用Muse才有效率。

Here's a list of ways I've used it so far. Maybe it'll give you some ideas.

下面是我到目前为止用过的一些方式。也许能给你点灵感。

I'm not affiliated with Meta by the way, just psyched about this tech. Whenever possible, I want to get things done in the digital realm just by interacting with my agent.

顺便说一句，我跟Meta没关系，就是单纯对这项技术很兴奋。只要有可能，我就想只通过跟我的代理互动，就把数字世界的事都办了。

It's definitely time investment upfront to get things set up and learn how to best work with Muse, but over time, it has been learning better and better how I like things done, and now it's starting to save me more and more time.

前期确实要花时间设置、学习怎么跟Muse配合最好，但时间一长，它越来越懂我喜欢怎么做事，现在开始帮我省越来越多时间了。

News alerts

新闻提醒

I have Muse monitor news topics I care about, like humanoid robotics. When significant developments happen, Muse reports back to me with a summary and sources.

我让Muse盯着我在乎的新闻话题，比如人形机器人。有重大进展时，Muse会给我发摘要和来源。

Booking

预订

I play tennis a lot. I ask Muse what courts are open, then I pick one and Muse books it and puts it on my calendar. Same deal for movie theater showtimes and tickets.

我经常打网球。我问Muse哪些场地空着，挑一个，Muse就帮我订好并加到日历里。电影院排片和买票也一样。

Restaurant reservations work wherever there's an online portal, and Muse is supposed to gain the ability to actually phone businesses soon.

餐厅订位只要有在线入口就能办，据说Muse很快还能直接打电话给商家。

Kanban

看板（日语外来词）

I wanted a custom project board to organize all my ideas and our shared projects, so I had Muse code one from scratch. No Kanban feature existed.

我想要一个自定义的项目板来整理我所有的想法和我们共同的项目，所以让Muse从头写了一个。当时根本没有看板功能。

I described what I wanted and Muse built it. Now I run all my projects and ideas through it, and when I want a tweak I ask Muse in plain language.

我描述了我想要什么，Muse就做出来了。现在我用它管理所有项目和想法，想调整就用大白话跟Muse说。

Calendar management

日历管理

I plugged Muse into my Google Calendar. The connector was broken for about a week, so I had Muse check every day until the fix landed.

我把Muse接进了我的谷歌日历。连接器坏了大概一周，我就让Muse每天检查，直到修好为止。

Now I add, edit, and delete events by voice, and Muse monitors my messages and email to update the calendar dynamically as things come in.

现在我可以用语音增删改日程，Muse还会盯着我的消息和邮件，有新情况就动态更新日历。

Spotify

Spotify（国外版网易云听歌应用）

I have Muse play songs on my phone or computer, or build me a playlist of songs I haven't heard before.

我让Muse在手机或电脑上放歌，或者给我建一个我没听过的歌单。

Texting

发短信

Muse can text individuals over SMS and RCS (no group texts on Android yet, that's a current limitation). My standing rule: Muse drafts, I approve, nothing sends without me seeing it first.

Muse可以通过SMS和RCS给个人发短信（安卓上还不能群发，这是目前的限制）。我的固定规则是：Muse起草，我批准，我没看过的东西绝不发出去。

I could give Muse full autonomy if I trusted it that far. I'm not there yet.

如果我足够信任它，可以给Muse完全自主权。但我还没到那一步。

Muse's phone number

Muse的手机号

I gave Muse a Google Voice number so Muse can text directly with my partner. If she can't reach me, Muse can tell her things like my phone battery status or share my location.

我给Muse配了一个Google Voice号码，这样它可以直接跟我伴侣发短信。如果她找不到我，Muse可以告诉她我的手机电量或者共享我的位置。

Voice notes

语音笔记

There's no real-time voice mode yet, so voice notes are mostly how I interact with Muse. I record a voice note, Muse transcribes it, and I can request that Muse reply in a voice note if I'm doing something else and would rather listen than read.

还没有实时语音模式，所以语音笔记是我跟Muse互动的主要方式。我录一段语音，Muse转成文字，如果我正在忙别的、想听不想看，还可以让Muse用语音回复。

I often do this to have Muse deep research a topic and then tell me about it while I drive.

我经常这样让Muse深入研究一个话题，然后在我开车的时候讲给我听。

Podcasts

播客

Muse can generate multi-voice podcast episodes debating any topic. I tried it once and it's not for me, I'd rather get a concise voice note. Mentioning it because the feature exists.

Muse可以生成多声音的播客节目，辩论任何话题。我试过一次，不适合我，我更喜欢简洁的语音笔记。提一下是因为这个功能存在。

Deep research

深度研究

I ask Muse about a topic and Muse goes deep: multiple sources, different opinions, then builds a proper report as an artifact with pictures, links, sources, and conclusions. As detailed as I want it.

我问Muse一个话题，它就会深挖：多个来源、不同观点，然后做成一份正经报告，带图片、链接、来源和结论。我想要多详细就多详细。

Monthly finances

月度财务

For about 25 years I've completed a monthly financial statement in Excel by hand. Took hours. Now I've connected Muse read-only to all my accounts through Plaid and Muse generates the whole statement.

大概25年来，我每个月都手动在Excel里做财务报表。要花好几个小时。现在我把Muse通过Plaid只读连接到所有账户，Muse直接生成整份报表。

Muse asks me about anything it can't see, like cash transactions. Fifteen minutes instead of hours. Next up is a custom interactive dashboard ala Monarch just for me.

Muse会问我它看不到的东西，比如现金交易。十五分钟搞定，以前要几个小时。下一步是做一个像Monarch那样的自定义交互式仪表盘，专门给我用。

Price alerts

价格提醒

I tell Muse: "Check every six hours and tell me if it moved more than 2% since you last reported." Works for crypto, stocks, etc.

我跟Muse说：“每六小时查一次，如果比上次报告涨跌超过2%就告诉我。”加密货币、股票什么的都行。

Email drafts

邮件草稿

Muse monitors my work inbox and drafts replies in my voice, trained on a lot of my past writing and responses. I review each draft, edit when I want, and Muse learns from my corrections over time. Drafts only, Muse never sends anything on its own.

Muse盯着我的工作收件箱，用我的语气起草回复，训练材料是我过去的很多写作和回复。我审每份草稿，想改就改，Muse会从我的修改中慢慢学习。只起草，Muse从不自己发东西。

YouTube comments

YouTube评论

Every Monday Muse scans one of my channels for new comments and drafts replies. I review them, give the go-ahead, and Muse posts them using its browser.

每周一Muse扫描我一个频道的新评论并起草回复。我审完批准，Muse用浏览器发出去。

Social posts

社交帖子

Still in testing. Muse drafts posts for my community page and X, and we co-write the promising ones together. Muse hasn't posted autonomously yet, but I think there's real potential here since I don't have time to write everything myself.

还在测试中。Muse为我的社区页面和X(Twitter改名后)起草帖子，有潜力的我们一起合写。Muse还没有自主发布过，但我觉得这里潜力很大，因为我没时间什么都自己写。

Bug monitoring

Bug监控

I have a mobile app with crash reporting through Sentry. My muse watches it, and when a new error surfaces Muse goes to my GitHub repo, figures out what went wrong, and drafts a fix before I even know there's a problem.

我有一个移动应用，通过Sentry做崩溃报告。我的Muse盯着它，有新错误出现时，Muse就去我的GitHub仓库，搞清楚哪里出问题，在我还不知道有毛病之前就起草好修复方案。

PC screen viewing

PC屏幕查看

There's a desktop app for Mac that gives Muse deeper access, but I'm on PC and Android, so no app for me. Instead Muse and I built a screenshot stream: my main monitor to Muse every few seconds, only when I turn it on.

Mac有个桌面应用能让Muse获得更深权限，但我用的是PC和安卓，没应用可用。于是我和Muse搭了个截屏流：我的主显示器每隔几秒发给Muse，只有我开启时才发。

When I'm lost in unfamiliar software I just ask where to click and Muse is right there looking over my shoulder.

当我搞不懂不熟悉的软件时，我就问该点哪里，Muse就在那儿看着我屏幕。

Managing other AIs

管理其他AI

I think of myself as president, Muse as vice president, and models like Claude Opus as employees. Muse holds all my context, goals, and plans, so Muse prompts the specialist models better and faster than I could myself.

我把自己当总统，Muse当副总统，Claude Opus这些模型当员工。Muse掌握我所有的背景、目标和计划，所以它给专业模型下指令比我更快更好。

Muse can also use API keys directly or talk to a self-hosted model if I give Muse that power.

如果我授权，Muse还可以直接用API密钥，或者跟自托管模型对话。

Media generation

媒体生成

Muse doesn't generate images or video itself, but Muse has access to Meta's models that do. Images are usually good enough for what I need. Video is rough, Muse won't keep character consistency. The content filters are aggressive too, I couldn't generate battle scenes with any violence. Useful to have, not great.

Muse自己不生成图片或视频，但能调用Meta的模型来做。图片通常够我用。视频比较粗糙，Muse保持不了角色一致性。内容过滤器也很严，我连带暴力的战斗场景都生成不了。有用，但不算好。

Google Drive collaboration

Google Drive协作（类似云端文档）

I love this one. Muse and I work in the same document live. I say "add a paragraph here" or "reformat these headers" and Muse does it while I watch. Kind of magic.

我最喜欢这个。Muse和我在同一个文档里实时协作。我说“这里加一段”或者“重新排版这些标题”，Muse就当着我的面做。有点像魔法。

I know Gemini is natively integrated, but Muse knows me much better and has access to all my context.

我知道Gemini是原生集成的，但Muse更了解我，能访问我所有的上下文。

D&D

龙与地下城

I'm a dungeon master. Muse has all my campaign notes and our house rules, so Muse helps me prep sessions and pivot mid-game when the players go somewhere I never planned for.

我是地下城主。Muse有我所有的战役笔记和我们的房规，所以Muse帮我准备团、在玩家跑偏到我没计划的地方时临场调整。

Muse could theoretically run the game itself, but without voice mode Muse isn't fast enough. I'm still the creative lead, Muse just makes me faster.

Muse理论上能自己带团，但没有语音模式，它不够快。我还是创意主导，Muse只是让我更快。

Video editing

视频剪辑

I handed Muse 90 minutes of tennis match footage and Muse cuts it down to just the points, no dead time between them. We're still refining this process and it's nowhere near as good as Swing Vision yet, but it's got potential.

我给了Muse 90分钟的网球比赛录像，Muse把它剪成只有得分回合，去掉中间的垃圾时间。我们还在改进这个流程，目前远不如Swing Vision，但有潜力。

Interesting side note: bulk compute like this barely seems to touch token usage. Spinning up the LLM costs tokens, but the heavy programmatic work on its VM apparently doesn't. I don't fully understand the boundary yet.

有意思的附带发现：这种大批量计算似乎几乎不消耗token。启动LLM要花token，但虚拟机上的繁重程序性工作好像不花。我还没完全搞懂边界在哪。

Dynamic outfits

动态装扮

Fun one to end on. Muse picks a new outfit on some days based on holidays and what's on my calendar. Tennis day gets a tennis outfit, the day I saw Resident Evil he dressed like a zombie apocalypse survivor. Just a fun way to keep it fresh.

最后一个好玩的。Muse有时候会根据节日和我日历上的安排换新装扮。网球日就穿网球装，我看《生化危机》那天他打扮成僵尸末日幸存者。就是好玩，保持新鲜感。
